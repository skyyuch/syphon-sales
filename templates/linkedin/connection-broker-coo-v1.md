# LinkedIn Connection Note — Broker COO / Head of Trading v1

**Audience**: COO / Head of Trading / Head of Execution at FX brokers
**Constraint**: 280 characters max (LinkedIn limit)
**Tone**: Specific, no pitch

---

## Variables
- `{{first_name}}`
- `{{observation}}` — 對方 profile / 公司近期動態

## Template

Hi {{first_name}}, {{observation}} caught my attention. I work on execution infrastructure (AI-native, FIX-native) for brokers running $500M+ daily. No pitch — just want to follow your work and trade notes occasionally.

---

## Observation examples
- "your post on B-book hedging logic"
- "your team's recent move into LATAM pairs"
- "your panel at iFX last year"
- "your background at [previous company]"

## Don't use generic openers
❌ "I'd love to connect"
❌ "Saw we have mutual connections"
❌ "Loved your profile"

## Follow-up after accept
Wait 2-3 days, then send InMail (separate template).
