# Cold Email — Generic Broker (English) v1

**Audience**: Retail / institutional FX brokers with $500M+ daily volume
**Use when**: 第一次接觸，無共同關係人
**Tone**: Short, data-led, no marketing fluff
**Send time**: Tue/Wed/Thu 09:00-11:00 local time

---

## Variables
- `{{first_name}}`
- `{{company}}`
- `{{specific_observation}}` — e.g. "your recent expansion to MENA", "your MT5 setup"
- `{{relevant_pain}}` — 對方類型對應的痛點
- `{{cta_link}}` — Calendly link or specific question
